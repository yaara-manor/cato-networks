# Accessing the Master Service Agreement

The Cato Master Service Agreement (MSA) defines the terms of the agreement made between Cato and each customer. You can access the agreement [here](https://www.catonetworks.com/msa/).

The first time that the initial administrator logs in to the Cato Management Application, there is a prompt to confirm the Cato MSA. In addition to describing the customer's obligations and limitations, the MSA includes the details of Cato's Service Level Agreement (SLA).
