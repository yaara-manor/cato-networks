# Cato Support Communication Methods and Contact Information

Security is at the heart of what we do at Cato Networks, and we are serious in our commitment to safeguarding customer information. Notably, we recognize the gravity of concerns such as phishing and the integrity of personally identifiable information (PII), which resonate deeply with our customers, partners, and, consequently, us.

To ensure clarity and transparency, our customers must be well-informed about the precise communication channels employed by Cato Support. We want our customers to understand that Cato Support will only communicate through specific communication paths.

Cato Support has various communication methods to reach our customers, including phone calls, the Cato Support Portal, and emails.

## Allowlisting Domains for Email Communication

> [!TIP]
> **IMPORTANT:**
> 
> You can't open Support tickets over email, please use the [AI Workspace](/v1/docs/getting-help-with-ask-ai) in the Cato Management Application (CMA) to open tickets (see below).

Cato Support uses specific email addresses under the catonetworks.com domain to communicate with customers. Please make sure to allowlist:

- `support@catonetworks.com`
- For our Support Services, also allowlist:
  - ILMM: `ilmm@catonetworks.com`
  - NOCaaS: `nocaas@catonetworks.com`

## Viewing Support Tickets

Customers can view their tickets in the [Support Portal](https://support.catonetworks.com) that requires authentication from the Cato Auth server at https://auth.catonetworks.com.

If a customer is concerned about a link sent to them, the best practice is to manually access the Support Portal by typing the URL ([https://support.catonetworks.com](https://support.catonetworks.com)) into the browser and validating the certificates.

For more information, see how to [submit a ticket](/v1/docs/submitting-a-support-ticket) via the the AI Workspace in the CMA.
