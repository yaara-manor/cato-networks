# Country's Allocation to License Groups and ZTNA Users

This article lists the countries that are included in the groups for Cato account terms and licenses.

Cato reserves the right, in its sole discretion, to amend the country list below, including but not limited to moving countries between groups and opening new groups such amendment shall only take effect upon renewal of Customer’s then current Subscription Term of the Solution or upgrade, or additional Solution subscribed for from Cato.

See Cato's MSA for the list of countries where the service may not be sold or delivered to [https://www.catonetworks.com/msa/](https://www.catonetworks.com/msa/).

## Overview of Licenses for ZTNA Users

These are the License Groups that are available for ZTNA users:

- General - worldwide except for China, Morocco, and Vietnam
- China
- Morocco
- Vietnam

**Note:** Purchase ZTNA (SDP) user licenses based on the user’s primary work location. For example, a user located in China requires a China ZTNA license and can continue to use that license when they travel to other countries. The primary work location isn't visible or configurable in the CMA.

## Overview of Site License Groups

These are the License Groups that are available for sites:

### Enforcement Model

> [!NOTE]
> Note
> 
> Cato account licenses use one of two models. This section applies to the **Enforcement Model** only and is not relevant to the **Bursting Model** (starting in January 2027). Not sure which license model your account uses? See [Identifying your License Model](/v1/docs/identifying-your-license-model).

- Group1
  - For individual Site licenses, the tiers in Mbps are: 25, 50, 100, 250, 500, 1000, 2000, 3000, 5000, and 10000
  - For Pooled licenses, the tiers start at 100 Mbps and go up in increments of 10. They can be distributed to individual sites in increments of 10 (e.g., 20, 50, 70, and not 23 or 75).
- Group2
  - For individual Site licenses, the tiers in Mbps are: 25, 50, 100, 250, 500, 1000, 2000, 3000, 5000, and 10000
  - For Pooled licenses, the tiers start at 100 Mbps and go up in increments of 10. They can be distributed to individual sites in increments of 10 (e.g., 20, 50, 70, and not 23 or 75).
  - You can apply Group 2 licenses to Group 1 countries, but not vice versa
- China

Tiers of 1Mbps increments (e.g. 1Mbps, 7Mbps, 19Mbps, or 72Mbps, and not 4.3 Mbps, 8.4 Mbps, 54.3 Mbps)
- Morocco

Tiers of 1Mbps increments (e.g. 1Mbps, 7Mbps, 19Mbps, or 72Mbps, and not 4.3 Mbps, 8.4 Mbps, 54.3 Mbps)
- Vietnam

Tiers of 1Mbps increments (e.g. 1Mbps, 7Mbps, 19Mbps, or 72Mbps, and not 4.3 Mbps, 8.4 Mbps, 54.3 Mbps)

### Bursting Model (Starting 2027)

> [!NOTE]
> Note
> 
> Cato account licenses use one of two models. This section applies to the **Bursting Model** (starting in January 2027) only and is not relevant to the **Enforcement Model** (starting in January 2027). Not sure which license model your account uses? See [Identifying your License Model](/v1/docs/identifying-your-license-model).

- Group 1:
  - For Pooled licenses, the tiers start at 1Mbps with tiers of 1Mbps increments (e.g., 2, 11, 54, 100, or 555, and not 4.3 Mbps, 1008.4)
- Group 2:
  - For Pooled licenses, the tiers start at 1Mbps with tiers of 1Mbps increments (e.g., 2, 11, 54, 100, or 555, and not 4.3 Mbps, 1008.4)
- China

Tiers of 1Mbps increments (e.g. 1Mbps, 7Mbps, 19Mbps, or 72Mbps, and not 4.3 Mbps, 8.4 Mbps, 54.3 Mbps)
- Morocco

Tiers of 1Mbps increments (e.g. 1Mbps, 7Mbps, 19Mbps, or 72Mbps, and not 4.3 Mbps, 8.4 Mbps, 54.3 Mbps)
- Vietnam

Tiers of 1Mbps increments (e.g. 1Mbps, 7Mbps, 19Mbps, or 72Mbps, and not 4.3 Mbps, 8.4 Mbps, 54.3 Mbps)
