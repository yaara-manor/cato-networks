# Customizing Email Notifications

The **Email Template** page lets Cato accounts customize the look and feel of email notifications that are sent to Cato Management Application admins. For example, email notifications that are sent for Socket updates and license changes. For information about email notifications, see [Account Level Alerts and System Notifications](/v1/docs/account-level-alerts-and-system-notifications).

These are the elements of email notifications that you can customize:

- **Email Logo** that is displayed on the email header
  - Supported file types: JPEG, PNG, and GIF
  - Maximum file size is 200KB
  - We recommend that you use logos with transparent background
- **Brand color** that is used for the theme of the email
- **Sender email** that the email is sent from, catonetworks.com or the account subdomain
- **Sender name** that the email is sent from
- **Customize contact us message** that is shown on the footer of the email

![Customize_Email.png](https://cdn.document360.io/81d0367c-2b69-4ba8-b412-1c8f46ce677e/Images/Documentation/24198329718045.png)

**To customize the email notifications for your account:**

1. From the navigation menu, select **Account > Email Template**.
2. Customize the elements for the notifications that are described above.
  - To upload a file for the **Logo**, click the image or drag-and-drop the file to the image.
3. Click **Save**.
4. To remove all the customized email settings and replace them with the standard Cato Networks settings, click **Restore defaults**.
