# Customizing Reports

This article discusses how to customize the Cato [reports](/v1/docs/cato-reports) with colors and logos that are consistent with your company's branding.

## Overview

You can configure the CMA so that your reports match your company branding and not Cato's. This gives the reports your company's look and feel, providing users with the familiarity and comfort of their recognized environment.

You can change the following branding settings:

- Report logo image
- Background image
- Text color for headings

## Customizing Cato Reports

These are the specifications for the background image:

- Supported file types: JPG, PNG, SVG (viewbox required)
- Maximum file size: 5MB
- Recommended size: 800 x 640 px
  - If your image has too high a resolution (length or width greater than 2500 px), unexpected errors may occur

These are the specifications for the logo:

- Supported file types: JPG, PNG, SVG (viewbox required)
- Maximum file size: 500KB
- Recommended logo size: 150 x 75 px
- We recommend that you use logos with transparent backgrounds

You can zoom in/out and crop background images after uploading them if the image is a JPG or PNG. SVG images are supported, but cannot be manipulated after uploading them.

**To customize Cato reports:**

1. From the navigation menu, select **Account > Reports Template.**
2. To upload a file for the **Report Logo**, click the image or drag and drop your file onto the image.
3. To upload a file for the **Background Image**, click the image or drag and drop your file onto the image. If the image is a JPG or PNG file, you can zoom in or crop the image after uploading.
4. To edit the **Text Color** of the headings, click the text color box and specify a different color.
5. Click **Save**.
6. To remove all the customized report settings and replace them with the standard Cato Networks settings, click **Restore defaults**.
