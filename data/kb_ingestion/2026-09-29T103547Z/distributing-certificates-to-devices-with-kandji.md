# Distributing Certificates to Devices with Kandji

This article explains how to distribute device certificates used for device checks to devices using Kandji

## Overview

You can distribute your corporate self-signed certificates, or the Cato root certificate to devices in your network using Kandji as your MDM. This streamlines the distribution of device certificates across devices. By managing certificate distribution through an MDM, you can centrally control certificate deployment, ensuring robust security measures are consistently enforced.

## Distributing Certificates to Devices

For more information about adding certificates to the Kandji library, see the [Kandji documentation for Library Overview](https://support.kandji.io/support/solutions/articles/72000558739-certificate-profile).

In addition, see the [Kandji documentation for Certificate Library Item](https://support.kandji.io/kb/certificate-library-item).
