# Finding the Public IP of Your Sites in the Cato Management Application

Your organization might have a lot of locations, and keeping track of their public IPs can sometimes get away from even diligent admins. This is especially true for sites with dynamic public IP addresses. Not to worry, Cato can help.

From the **Home > Topology** page, the public IP from the ISP is visible in the right side pane when the site is selected, like so:
