# Getting Started with AI Security

## Overview

The AI Security Platform lets you securely adopt and use AI across your organization without exposing sensitive data or increasing risk. It provides visibility and control over every AI interaction - whether employees are using public AI tools, accessing enterprise applications with embedded AI features, or building custom AI-powered applications. You can enforce consistent security policies, prevent data leakage, and detect misuse in real time as AI usage expands across your environment.

The platform is structured around two core domains: AI for End Users and AI for Applications. AI for End Users focuses on governing how employees interact with third-party AI services and AI-enabled SaaS applications. It gives you the ability to monitor prompts and responses, apply data protection controls, enforce acceptable use policies, and maintain compliance. This ensures that innovation and productivity gains from AI tools do not come at the expense of security or regulatory requirements.

AI for Applications extends protection to AI capabilities embedded within enterprise applications and custom-built AI services. It helps you secure API calls, manage data flows between applications and AI models, and enforce policies for internally developed AI solutions. By combining visibility, policy enforcement, and threat prevention across both domains, the AI Security Platform enables you to confidently scale AI adoption while maintaining full control of your security posture.
