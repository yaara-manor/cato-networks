# Getting Started with MDR

Cato's Managed Detection and Response (MDR) service offers continuous threat detection and guidance to help you quickly and effectively respond to malicious events and security incidents. The MDR service leverages artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning (ML) tools, combined with our expert Security team to investigate and verify threats, alert our customers, reduce risk of breach, and improves the security posture.

The Detection & Response page in the Cato Management Application lets you analyze stories for threats and drill-down to investigate the relevant data for the story. For more information, see [Reviewing Detection & Response Stories for MDR Customers](/v1/docs/reviewing-detection-response-stories-for-mdr-customers).
