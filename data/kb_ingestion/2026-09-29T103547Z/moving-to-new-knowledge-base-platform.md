# Moving to New Knowledge Base Platform

Cato is moving the Knowledge Base to a new platform to provide a more intuitive and efficient enablement and knowledge experience. This article explains the improvements and changes. There is no impact on opening or viewing Support tickets.

## Overview

The new Knowledge Base platform includes several significant improvements:

- Search capabilities are enhanced with filtering options, so you can narrow results and find the most relevant content more easily
- Easier navigation between articles related to the same feature
- Publish multiple articles to a single PDF

## Moving to the New Platform

Once generally available, the new platform will host all existing Cato feature content. To ensure a successful transition, articles will gradually move to the new platform in stages during an early availability phase. Until the transition is complete, the new platform will be hosted at a temporary URL.

- During early availability (EA), the new platform will be hosted at a new domain
  - All content will be duplicated for the current and new platforms
- No impact to Support tickets, or the related URLs
- Any bookmarked KB articles and links will continue to work even after moving to the new platform

### Early Availability of the New Platform

EA of the new Knowledge Base platform starts on July 19, 2026. The new platform is hosted at a new domain, [knowledge.catonetworks.com](https://knowledge.catonetworks.com/), and all articles will be updated on both the old and the new platform. **IMPORTANT:** If you follow an article in the current Knowledge Base, the follow setting does not carry over to the new Knowledge Base platform. During EA, make sure to follow articles in the new platform. See [below](/v1/docs/moving-to-new-knowledge-base-platform#how-do-i-know-which-articles-i-am-following-in-the-old-platform).

### General Availability

We are planning to release the new Knowledge Base platform as General Availability (GA) starting Aug 9, 2026. Individual articles will have new URLs. If you access an old article URL, you are automatically redirected to the new location.

## How do I know the Exact Dates for Migration to the New Platform?

We are planning to release the new Knowledge Base platform as General Availability (GA) starting Aug 9, 2026. A banner is shown in the old Knowledge Base announcing the change.

## Open or Track Support Tickets

To open a new Support ticket, go to the [CMA Ask AI Workspace](https://externallink.cc.catonetworks.com/#/account/me/aiWorkspace?support).

View existing tickets [here](https://support.catonetworks.com/hc/en-us/requests).

There is no impact on Support ticket activity during all rollout phases, including EA and GA.

## How do I know which Articles I am Following in the Old Platform?

To show the articles that you are following in the old Knowledge Base platform:

1. Sign in to [support.catonetworks.com](https://support.catonetworks.com)
2. Click the menu in the upper-right corner and select **Activities**.
3. Click the **Following** tab. The page shows the articles and sections that you are following.
