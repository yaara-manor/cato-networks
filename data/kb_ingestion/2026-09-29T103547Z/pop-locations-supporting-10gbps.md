# PoP Locations Supporting 10Gbps

This article lists the PoP locations in the Cato Cloud that are immediately available to support 10Gbps site throughput. Click **Follow** to automatically receive email notifications for updates to this article.

Socket X1700B and Cloud Interconnect sites fully support 10Gbps connection speeds for WAN traffic (Off-Cloud and bypass traffic are not supported for 10Gbps connection speeds).

Cato recommends configuring Sockets in Active-Active mode, with each interface set up for 5Gbps downstream and 5Gbps upstream.

To reach the maximum throughput of 10Gbps, you need to have multiple hosts and flows.

Other PoP locations support a maximum site throughput of 5Gbps.

## Available

The following PoP locations support 10Gbps throughput.

| Region | PoP Location |
| --- | --- |
| United States | - Ashburn, Ashburn_DC2 - Atlanta - Chicago - Dallas - Houston - Los Angeles - New York - Santa Clara - Seattle |
| EMEA | - Amsterdam - Frankfurt, Frankfurt_DC2 - London - Manchester - Marseille - Milan - Paris |
| Japan | - Tokyo, Tokyo_DC2, Tokyo_DC3, Tokyo_DC4 - Osaka, Osaka_DC2 |
