# Product Update - December 16, 2024

## New Features & Enhancements

There are no new features or enhancements for the Cato service for this week. Take a look at these previously released features:

- **Review Recommended Best Practices for your Account:** The [Monitoring > Best Practices page](/v1/docs/reviewing-posture-checks-for-your-account) shows if you are meeting Cato's recommended configurations and settings for your optimal performance and security in your account. For example, the page shows whether risky apps are being blocked and can identify security rules that are too permissive.
- **Natural Language Search for Events:** The Cato Management Application integrates generative AI with the Events page to provide [natural language search](/v1/docs/using-natural-language-search) to let you drill down and identify the events that contain the relevant data you need. For example, "Show me Internet firewall security events from phishing category URLs" is converted to a search query to show you the phishing-related events.
