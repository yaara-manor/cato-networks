# Product Update - December 23, 2024

## New Features and Enhhancements

- **API Support for BGP Peering:** We added API support for [BGP configuration and monitoring](https://api.catonetworks.com/documentation/#query-site.bgpPeer) to enhance your control over site connectivity:
  - **Mutation APIs**:
    - addBgpPeer
    - updateBgpPeer
    - removeBgpPeer
  - **Query APIs**:
    - bgpPeer
    - siteBgpStatus
