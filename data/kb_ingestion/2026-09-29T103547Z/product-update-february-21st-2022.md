# Product Update - February 21st, 2022

## New Features & Enhancements

- **End-of-Life for Legacy Cato Management Application:** Starting on April 24, 2022, you will only be able to access all monitoring dashboards, events, and configurations in the [new Cato Management Application](https://cc.catonetworks.com/#/login). After this date, the legacy Cato Management Application will be end-of-life and no longer available.
