# Product Update - June 27th, 2022

## New Features & Enhancements

- **DHCP Lease Time Configuration:** For network ranges that use Cato as the DHCP server, you can configure the DHCP lease time per network range or for the entire account. [Read more](/v1/docs/configuring-dhcp-settings).
  - The feature will be available starting on July 3rd, 2022
- **Enhancements to the Cato Management Application:**
  - Edit filters in the Events screen
  - For SDP users, you can drill down and show the events pre-filtered for a specific SDP user
  - Notifications also support search functionality and improved overall usability

## Cato SDP Client Releases

- **iOS Client v4.4.3:** You can now download iOS Client version 4.4.3 from the iOS App Store. This version includes:
  - Support for landscape mode with iPads
  - Stabilization fixes for Office Mode
