# Product Update - Mar. 25th, 2024

## New Features & Enhancements

- **Assign Sockets Using the Socket Inventory Page:** Assign Sockets to the appropriate sites with the **Assign to a site** action in the Socket Inventory page. This simplifies the process of assigning several Sockets to different sites.
  - The Socket must be connected to the Internet with the status **Installed**
- **New Default Configuration for EPP Behavioral Engine:** We have enhanced Endpoint Protection (EPP) to ensure malicious processes are killed by default. We changed the configuration of the Behavioral engine from **Monitor** to **Kill** in the default [EPP profile](/v1/docs/configuring-endpoint-protection).
  - This change is for new EPP customers only, there's no impact to existing EPP customers
  - We recommend updating your existing configuration for the Behavioral engine to **Kill**
- **Better Visibility and Control over Access to your Account:** The new Administration > Account Access page gives you improved visibility and control over access to your account by Cato admins.
  - View active and recent admin access to your account, for example, to see the lifecycle for a Support ticket
  - Provide temporary access to your Cato account with specific permissions and end date, for example, for a Cato Professional Services engineer
- **Cato Management Application Enhancement:** For the Monitoring > Network Dashboard page, you can now group links by the service **Provider** with the **Group By** option (shown in the Links section). This gives you information about connectivity for all of your links for each **Provider**.
- **New Knowledge Base Page about the Cato Product Roadmap:** The [Cato Product Roadmap page](https://support.catonetworks.com/hc/en-us/articles/14517158733853-Cato-s-Upcoming-Features) lets you follow the status of features and enhancements that are currently in development and being rolled out.
  - The Roadmap is updated weekly, check the comments for information about the changes
  - Click **Follow** to automatically receive email notifications for updates to this page
  - You must be logged in to the Knowledge Base to access the Roadmap **Note:** Content described in this update is gradually rolled out to the Cato PoPs over a two-week period. In addition, new features are gradually activated in the Cato Management Application over the same two-week rollout period as the PoPs. For more information, see this [article](/v1/docs/understanding-rollout-to-the-cato-cloud-1). See the [Cato Status Page](https://status.catonetworks.com/) for more information about the planned maintenance schedule.
