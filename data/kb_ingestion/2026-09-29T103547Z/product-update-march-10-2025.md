# Product Update - March 10, 2025

## New Features & Enhancements

**In Case You Missed It**

**There are no new features or enhancements for the Cato service for this week. Take a look at these previously released features:**

- **Optimize Firewall Rules with AI-Driven Analysis and Insights:** This AI-driven [Internet Firewall policy](/v1/docs/what-is-the-cato-internet-firewall) enhancement provides insights to improve security posture and meet best practices. The Autonomous Firewall engine automatically analyzes your Internet Firewall rulebase and detects issues such as:
  - Temporary rules
  - Rules that are expired or soon to expire
  - Test rules
- **New Article about App Control & Data Protection for Sharepoint:** The Cato service can provide inline and out-of-band [app control (CASB)](/v1/docs/application-control-casb) and [Data Protection (DLP)](/v1/docs/data-loss-prevention-dlp) for a range of apps. We published a [new article](/v1/docs/protecting-sharepoint-with-app-control-data-protection) explaining how to protect your SharePoint tenant and prevent unauthorized data access and leakage.

## PoP Announcements

- **Houston, US**: A new range (199.27.33.0/24) is now available for the Houston PoP location.

- **Marseille, FR**: A new range (216.252.182.0/24) is now available for the Marseille PoP location.

- **Tokyo, JP**: A new range (123.253.154.0/24) will soon be added to the Tokyo PoP location.

- **New Localized Range for Albania (AL):** A new localized IP range (216.252.183.0/28) for Albania (serviced through the Milan PoP location) is now available.
