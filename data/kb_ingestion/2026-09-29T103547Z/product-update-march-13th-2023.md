# Product Update - March 13th, 2023

## New Features & Enhancements

- **User Awareness Supports SCIM provisioning for all IdPs:** Over the next few weeks, Cato’s Identity Agent will support [User Awareness for users provisioned with SCIM](https://support.catonetworks.com/hc/en-us/articles/7784362141085-Using-Cato-Identity-Agents-for-User-Awareness) as follows:
  - **Azure AD:**
    - Windows Clients v5.4 and macOS Clients v5.3 (and higher)
  - **Other IdPs:**
    - Windows Clients v5.5 and macOS Clients v5.3 (and higher)
- **Custom Branding for Windows Client:** You can upload a [custom logo](/v1/docs/customizing-the-cato-client) that is displayed in Windows Clients version 5.6 or higher.
  - For resellers, your logo appears in the Windows Clients of your managed accounts
  - Managed accounts can override the reseller logo and upload their own custom branding
