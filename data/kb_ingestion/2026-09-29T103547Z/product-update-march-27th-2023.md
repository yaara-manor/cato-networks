# Product Update - March 27th, 2023

## New Features & Enhancements

- **Research Threats with Cato’s Threat Catalog:** Search for information about threats and vulnerabilities, and learn more about how a specific threat is covered by Cato’s Security services. The [Threat Catalog](/v1/docs/using-the-threat-catalog) provides details for each threat, and you can also drill-down to further investigate specific items.
  - Filter for new items, and according to Cato security service or MITRE ATT&CK® technique
- **Change the Email Address of Existing SDP Users:** You can now [change the email address of SDP users](/v1/docs/changing-the-email-address-or-user-principal-name-of-users) imported to your account from an LDAP or SCIM Provider.
