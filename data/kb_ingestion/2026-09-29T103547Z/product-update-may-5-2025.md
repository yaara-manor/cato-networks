# Product Update - May 5, 2025

## New Features & Enhancements

- **New Report for GenAI Usage:** We are adding a new report that provides insights and highlights risks related to [GenAI app usage](/v1/docs/generating-a-genai-report). You can generate it on demand or schedule it to run automatically from the Home > Reports page.

## PoP Announcements

- New ranges are available in these PoP locations:
  - **Amsterdam, NL:** 216.252.186.0/24
  - **Brussels, BE:** 216.252.189.0/24
  - **Paris, FR:** 216.252.190.0/24
