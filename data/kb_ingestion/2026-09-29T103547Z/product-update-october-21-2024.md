# Product Update - October 21, 2024

There are no new features or enhancements for the Cato service for this week. Take a look at these previously released features:

- **Use XDR to Investigate Risky Sign-In Incidents:** We integrated Microsoft [Entra ID Protection alerts](/v1/docs/microsoft-entra-id-configuring-the-xops-integration) with Cato XDR to create stories that focus on risky sign-ins. This lets analysts investigate the issue within the broader context provided in the [XDR Stories Workbench](/v1/docs/reviewing-detection-response-xops-stories-in-the-stories-workbench).
  - For more information, see this [video](https://academy.catonetworks.com/xdr-entra-id-stories-1)
- **Custom Branding Options for your Cato Account:** Cato lets you customize various aspects of the platform to suit your company’s look and feel and create a unified branding experience. You can manage all aspects of the customization using the Administration > Branding section, which lets you configure the following:
  - [Management application](/v1/docs/customizing-the-cma)
  - [Prompt and Block pages](/v1/docs/customizing-the-warning-block-page-branding)
  - [Cato Client](/v1/docs/customizing-the-cato-client)
  - [Notification email templates](/v1/docs/customizing-email-notifications)
  - [Application Portal for Browser Access](/v1/docs/customizing-browser-application-portal)

Go to the [Cato Product Roadmap](https://bit.ly/49aFEKU) in the Knowledge Base to follow the status of upcoming features and enhancements.
