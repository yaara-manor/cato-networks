# Product Update - September 5th, 2022

## New Features & Enhancements

- **Exporting SDP Users Shows Client Version and Device Information:** When you export SDP Users in the Access > Users screen, the CSV files now includes the data for the **Device** and **Client Version**. [Read more](/v1/docs/exporting-user-data).
