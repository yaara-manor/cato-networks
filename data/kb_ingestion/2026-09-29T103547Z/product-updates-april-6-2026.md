# Product Updates - April 6, 2026

## New Features & Enhancements

- **New Android Client v5.5:** During the week of April 5, 2026, [Android Client version 5.5](/v1/docs/summary-of-cato-android-client-releases) will be uploaded to the Google Play Store. This version includes:
  - Stability improvements
  - Security updates
  - Bug fixes

- **Microsoft Exchange App Activities Integration Enhancement:** The [Microsoft Exchange App Activities](/v1/docs/microsoft-apps-including-copilot-configuring-the-app-activities-integrations) connector now captures email recipient information for sent messages, providing enhanced visibility into email communication flows.
  - To enable, log in to your Microsoft Entra admin center and add the Graph Application permission (<kbd>SecurityAnalyzedMessage.Read.All</kbd>) to the integration’s application
  - Requires a CASB license

- **NOCaaS Support for Account Operations Stories:** To expand the service scope for monitoring and analysis across your account, we added more account-level operational issues with new [stories](https://nocaas.catonetworks.com/hc/en-us/articles/10112444143261-Network-Operations-Center-as-a-Service-Supported-Stories) to Network Operations as a Service (NOCaaS).
  - The NOCaaS team manages Response Policy updates, and all changes generate audit logs
  - Requires a NOCaaS license

- **Browser Extension v1.7:** During the week of April 5, 2026, a [new Browser Extension version 1.7](/v1/docs/summary-of-browser-extension-releases) will be available in the Chrome Web Store, and includes new enhancements and bug fixes.
