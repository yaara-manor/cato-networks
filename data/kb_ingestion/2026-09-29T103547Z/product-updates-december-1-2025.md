# Product Updates - December 1, 2025

## New Features & Enhancements

- **CMA Ask AI Gives Account-Specific Answers:** You can now use the [**Ask AI**](/v1/docs/what-is-cato-s-ask-ai-agent) tool in the Cato Management Application (CMA) to get real-time and historical insights into your sites, users, applications, devices, bandwidth, and overall network health.
  - Ask questions such as “Which sites used the most bandwidth yesterday?” or “How many users are connected right now?”
  - Automatically determines whether to give an account-specific or general answer
  - These advanced capabilities are now available as part of a free trial
  - Click [here](https://academy.catonetworks.com/catos-ai-assistant) to watch a video recording of this feature
- **New CMA Demo Account:** Demo Mode gives you instant access to a Cato-maintained [demo account](/v1/docs/cato-s-demo-account) with sample data, a fully licensed environment that includes all Cato services.
  - Explore new features, review configurations, and experience the platform as if it were a live account
  - An in-app guide overlay is available to walk you through key capabilities with self-guided tours
  - Currently, the demo account is only available for customers running the CMA in the default EU region
  - To start exploring, go to Ask AI > Demo Mode
- **Track the Status of Configuration Changes:** View the real-time status of [changes to policies](/v1/docs/visibility-for-publishing-cma-policies) as they are applied to your account. This provides clear visibility into when the changes are fully deployed and actively impacting traffic in your network.
  - Displayed in the notifications area at the top of the CMA in the **My Policy Changes** tab
- **Improved Visibility for App Usage:** We’ve introduced new **Cloud Apps Overview** and **Inventory** [pages](/v1/docs/using-the-applications-dashboard) to replace the Cloud Apps dashboard. The new pages provide complete visibility and control over SaaS app usage and risk. Gain insights into how cloud apps are used across your organization, who is accessing them, and what data is being shared, all from a unified dashboard.
  - **Discover Shadow IT:** Identify unsanctioned applications and risky usage patterns
  - **Monitor & Secure SaaS Usage:** Track app activity to safeguard corporate environments
  - **Protect Sensitive Data:** Detect data exposure across SaaS platforms
  - **Investigate in Depth:** Use the integrated app inventory to analyze app metadata, user access, and data-sharing trends
  - CASB license required
  - Click [here](https://academy.catonetworks.com/application-overview-dashboard-inventory) to watch a video recording of this feature
- **Android Client v5.3:** The new Client will be available to download from the Google Play Store the week of November 30, 2025. This version includes:
  - Stability improvements
  - Security updates
  - Bug fixes
- **Device Posture Profile Included in Device Inventory:** For improved visibility into device posture and how it affects access decisions, a device’s Posture Profile is displayed in the [Device Inventory](/v1/docs/what-is-device-inventory).
  - The Device Posture Profile is included in the **Quick View** panel after you click a device
  - Requires IoT/OT Security license
