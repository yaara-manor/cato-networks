# Product Updates - December 15, 2025

## New Features & Enhancements

- **Windows Client v5.21:** Starting the week of December 14, 2025, we are rolling out the new Windows Client version 5.21. This version includes:
  - Improvements to the look and feel of the Client tray icon
  - Stability improvements
  - Security updates
  - Bug fixes

## In Case You Missed It

Take a look at this previously released feature:

- **Overview Dashboard Shows Key Account Metrics:** Gain immediate visibility into your account's overall health and performance to help you monitor issues and make informed decisions in real time. The [Account Overview dashboard](/v1/docs/using-the-overview-dashboard) consolidates key metrics including: network health, security posture, site status, and usage trends.
  - Includes high-level insights for [Experience Monitoring](/v1/docs/using-the-experience-monitoring-page), the Stories dashboard, and the [Device dashboard](/v1/docs/using-the-device-dashboard) for all customers
  - Click [here](https://academy.catonetworks.com/customer-overview-page) to watch a video recording of this feature
