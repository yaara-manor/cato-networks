# Selecting the Site Type

Each site in your account can be classified as one of these site types:

- Branch
- Headquarters
- Cloud Data Center
- Data Center

The site types are used to determine which icon is used for the site in the Home > Topology page. You can use them to easily identify the key sites in your account. The different site types are not functionally different in the Cato Management Application or your network.
