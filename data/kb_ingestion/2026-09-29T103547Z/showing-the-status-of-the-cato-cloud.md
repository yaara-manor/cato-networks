# Showing the Status of the Cato Cloud

Cato's Status Page ([https://status.catonetworks.com/](https://status.catonetworks.com/)) shows the status of the Cato Cloud and also notifications for planned maintenance and upgrades. This page shows real-time information about the Points of Presence (PoPs) in the Cato Cloud and also for the servers that host the Cato Management Application.

Maintenance for PoPs in the Cato Cloud help to provide the best possible infrastructure for your business and doesn't require any action on your part. During the maintenance window, actual maintenance operation takes about 30 seconds to complete.
