# Socket X1500 | Status LEDs for Ethernet Ports

**Front Panel:**

Power LED:

| **Color** | **Status** | **Location** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Green | Power ON | Upper LED |

HDD LED:

| **Color** | **Status** | **Location** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Red | Data access | Lower LED |

LAN1, LAN2, WAN1 and WAN2 LEDs:

| **Color** | **Indication** | **Status** | **Location** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Green | ON | Interface Link | Upper LED |
| Green | FLASHING | Interface Active | Upper LED |

| **Color** | **Status** | **Location** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Amber | 1000M | Lower LED |
| Green | 100M | Lower LED |
| Off | 10M or no link | Lower LED |

**Back Panel:**

LAN1, LAN2, WAN1 and WAN2 LEDs:

| **Color** | **Indication** | **Status** | **Location** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Green | ON | Interface Link | Left LED |
| Green | FLASHING | Interface Active | Left LED |

| **Color** | **Status** | **Location** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Amber | 1000M | Right LED |
| Green | 100M | Right LED |
| Off | 10M or no link | Right LED |
