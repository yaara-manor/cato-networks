# Upcoming Maintenance for the EPP Page in the CMA

On Sunday, Aug 23, Cato will be performing maintenance on the Endpoint Protection page in the Cato Management Application (CMA). During the maintenance window for your region, the **Security > Endpoint Protection** page in the CMA will be unavailable for up to 30 minutes.

This maintenance does not impact security protection from deployed Endpoint Protection (EPP) agents. Your EPP agents remain connected and continue to protect devices during this time. EPP events continue to be generated and will be shown in the CMA after the maintenance is completed.

**Note:** During the maintenance window, or for part of the maintenance window, new EPP agents may be unable to register.

## Maintenance Windows

| CMA Region | Maintenance Window (UTC) |
| --- | --- |
| Americas | 06:00-09:00 |
| EMEA + APAC (excluding Japan) | 14:00-18:00 |
| Japan | 15:00-18:00 |

## What is the Impact to the Account?

The following temporary impacts can occur during the maintenance window for your region:

- The Endpoint Protection page in the CMA is unavailable for up to 30 minutes
- New EPP agents may be unable to register
- EPP security events continue to be generated and saved in the account Data Lake

## What Action Do I Need to Take?

No action is required. The EPP service in the CMA will automatically resume in the CMA when the maintenance is complete.

If you are deploying new EPP agents during the maintenance window and registration is unsuccessful, retry the registration after the maintenance window ends.

## Who Do I Talk to If I Have Questions?

Please use the [Cato Ask AI agent](https://externallink.cc.catonetworks.com/#/account/me/aiWorkspace?support) in the CMA to answer questions about EPP, and you can [read articles](https://knowledge.catonetworks.com/docs/endpoint-protection) in the Knowledge Base.
