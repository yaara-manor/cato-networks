# Viewing the Account Info

This article describes the fields in the Account Info page for your Cato Management Application account.

You can show the following details of your account:

- **Account name** - The name of your Cato account in the Cato Management Application
- **Account ID** - Unique Cato ID for your account
- **Created on** - Date that the Cato Management Application account was created
- **Description** - Editable free text field

**To view the Account Info page:**

- From the navigation menu, click **Account > Account Info**.
