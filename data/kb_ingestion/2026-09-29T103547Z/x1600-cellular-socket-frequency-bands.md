# X1600 Cellular Socket Frequency Bands

## X1600 LTE Socket Frequency Bands

These are the supported frequency bands for the X1600 LTE Socket cellular links:

- LTE-FDD Bands: B1, B2, B3, B4, B5, B7, B8, B12, B13, B14, B17, B18, B19, B20, B25, B26, B28, B29, B30, B32, B66
- LTE-TDD Bands: B38, B39, B40, B41, B42, B43, B46, B48
- WCDMA Bands: B1, B2, B3, B4, B5, B6, B8, B19

## X1600 5G Socket Frequency Bands

These are the supported frequency bands for the X1600 5G Socket cellular links:

### LTE Bands

- B1, B2(B25), B3, B4(B66), B26(B5, B18, B19), B7, B8, B12(B17), B13, B14, B20, B28, B29(DL), B30, B32(DL), B34, B38, B39, B40, B41, B42, B43, B46(LAA), B48(CBRS), B66, B71

### 5G Bands

- n1, n2, n3, n5, n7, n8, n12, n13, n14, n18, n20, n25, n26, n28, n29, n30, n38, n40, n41, n48, n66, n71, n75, n76, n77, n78, n79
