# X1600 Socket Series Electrical Specifications

This article shows the details for the power consumption details and electrical specifications for Cato X1600 Sockets.

## X1600 Socket Power Specifications

### BTU/HR Details

- Idle - 16
- Full loading - 41

### Power Consumption (W)

- Max power - 34W
- Idle power - 14.3W

## X1600 Socket Power Supply Specifications

This specification defines the input, output characteristics and performance requirements for 24 watts switching mode AC to DC adapter.

- Power supply input
  - Voltage: 90-264 VAC
  - Frequency: 50-60 Hz
  - Current: 0.6 A max across voltage range
  - Protection: Undervoltage shutdown/retry (brownout)

- X1600 Socket input (power supply output)
  - Voltage: 12 VDC +/- 5%
  - Ripple: 120 mV pk-pk
  - Load current: 5.0 A max
  - Protection: Overcurrent shutdown/retry (short circuit)
